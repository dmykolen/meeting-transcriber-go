  median    how far apart the two put a word, in seconds

  lean      signed median: negative is early, which is the drift

  current   rows built from raw token times — what ships
  segments  one row per whisper segment, nothing split
  remap     split as now, token times stretched onto the segment's span
  split     whisper.cpp splits the segments (max_len 90, on word)
  novad     no VAD at all, so token times need no mapping
  plain     one row per segment, and no per-token times asked for at all
