EXPERIMENT D — a switch instead of a sum
recording 74 — a planning meeting, 1380.2 s

Two numbers decide this:
  ECHO  how loud the leftover copy of the far side is, relative to them.
        Under -25 dB nobody hears it. At -13 dB everybody does.
  YOU   how much of your own voice survives, where only you are talking.
        0 dB is untouched. Anything under -3 dB is a recording that lost you.

── fold as it ships (duck 0.25)
   sum: system + mic*0.25
   ECHO  median  -19.1 dB   p75  -13.5   p90   -7.9   audible in 1553 of 1574 (99%)
   YOU   -1.8 dB ← noticeably quieter than you were

── fold, ducked to 0.05
   same sum, twenty times quieter a second copy
   ECHO  median  -20.5 dB   p75  -14.6   p90   -8.7   audible in 1536 of 1574 (98%)
   YOU   -1.8 dB ← noticeably quieter than you were

── fold, ducked to 0.02
   same sum, near enough to a switch
   ECHO  median  -21.4 dB   p75  -15.5   p90   -9.2   audible in 1506 of 1574 (96%)
   YOU   -1.8 dB ← noticeably quieter than you were

── switch, soft
   one owner per instant, ramped
   ECHO  median   -8.7 dB   p75   -2.8   p90    1.3   audible in 1405 of 1574 (89%)
   YOU   -0.0 dB ← you come through as recorded

── switch, hard
   one owner per instant, no ramp
   ECHO  median   -9.1 dB   p75   -3.0   p90    1.0   audible in 1393 of 1574 (89%)
   YOU   +0.0 dB ← you come through as recorded

── switch, soft, tap 200 ms earlier
   aligned, then one owner per instant
   ECHO  median  -16.7 dB   p75   -8.4   p90   -2.4   audible in 1173 of 1579 (74%)
   YOU   -0.0 dB ← you come through as recorded

── switch, soft, tap 230 ms earlier
   aligned, then one owner per instant
   ECHO  median  -23.3 dB   p75  -13.8   p90   -7.1   audible in 877 of 1589 (55%)
   YOU   -0.0 dB ← you come through as recorded

── switch, soft, tap 260 ms earlier
   aligned, then one owner per instant
   ECHO  median  -15.1 dB   p75   -7.2   p90   -2.5   audible in 1257 of 1583 (79%)
   YOU   -0.0 dB ← you come through as recorded


What to read from this: ducking harder buys decibels of echo at the price
of your own voice, because it is still a sum. A switch pays neither price,
because at any instant only one channel is in the mix at all.
